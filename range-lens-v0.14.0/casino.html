Table rules & payouts

Six shuffled decks per round. Dealer checks for blackjack before you act and stands on soft 17. A natural blackjack returns your stake plus 3:2 winnings. Regular wins pay 1:1; pushes return your stake.

Double on any first two cards, including after a split, when you can afford the extra stake. Doubling deals exactly one card and ends that hand.

Split two cards of the same rank into equal stakes, up to four hands. Play each hand in turn. Split aces receive one card each and cannot be resplit. A 21 after a split pays 1:1, not blackjack.

No insurance or surrender. Chip denominations scale with your available coins. The smallest chip stays at 2; larger chips follow roughly 10%, 20%, and 50% of your wallet, rounded to convenient values. Unaffordable chips are disabled. Selecting a chip never places a bet by itself. Even coin bets keep all payouts in whole coins. Stakes are deducted as you bet; coins returned include your stake. An unfinished round is saved when you leave, and resumes on return.

These are fictional fishing coins with no cash value. If you run out, the pond and free earthworms are always there.
