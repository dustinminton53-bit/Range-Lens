How to fish

Hold the cast button or Space to build distance; release to send your bobber out. Tiny nibbles are a tease. When the bobber dips and Fish on! appears, press to hook.

Hold to reel; release when tension gets high. A snapping line loses the fish. Landed catches go into your basket automatically. Sell them for coins, then choose bait and rods at the tackle shop.

Longer casts reach different fish. Better rods make strong pulls easier. Worms are unlimited; purchased bait is used on each cast. Nothing here uses market data or real money.
